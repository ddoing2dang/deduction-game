## Measured architectural changes

- The original decorative double door is centred at Blender X `-4.2` on the hall wall Y `-3.5`. The new exterior portal is at Y `-12.3`, measured from the actual kitchen frontage. Both share exactly the same entrance axis.
- New walls are 0.26 m thick and 4.35 m high, with approximately 2.64 m clear width. The rear splays into the existing broad door surround. The exterior mouth has real extruded arch geometry, piers, stone caps and brass accents.
- A single continuous marble floor mesh runs from the portal apron through the rear splay to the door threshold. A continuous central runner and narrow borders replace the old short runner. Symmetric wall lamps, wainscot, mouldings and a light ivory coffered crossover canopy complete the enclosure.
- External urns move to the portal margins. Only the exterior door sconces shift slightly inward to clear the splayed walls; the decorative door location, role and lack of interaction are unchanged.
- The 18 legacy bridge railing render components are hidden; their original navigation/collision assets are retained. The bridge floor stays in its original location and is covered by the new render floor in the entrance composition.

## Essential collision distinction

The existing `DOOR_04_05` service bridge crosses this entrance axis at the same elevation. A fully colliding sealed wall across that crossing would break a LOCK route. Therefore:

- Eleven separate wall/overhead boundary meshes have non-trigger MeshColliders and the `MansionEntranceBoundary` marker, under the exterior hierarchy rather than the gameplay connections. They block the non-crossing side exits and are marked Not Walkable for NavMesh collection.
- The protected crossover remains physically open. Two **0.26 m thick visual infill wall units** close its side sightlines in entrance views. `MansionEntranceScreen` cuts them away before an actual character reaches the measured bridge region, and hides them with side/action exterior presentation. These units have no collider, DoorID, trigger or interaction.
- The floor is render-only and does not create a new walkable NavMesh surface, RoomNode or transition. The decorative double door remains noncolliding and noninteractive. This entrance is architectural presentation, not a new playable map connection.
- Existing 4-to-5 floor, entries, original collision boundaries and movement logic are unchanged. All 11 routes are tested in both directions, including per-frame checks that the infill is hidden during actual 4-to-5 traversal.
